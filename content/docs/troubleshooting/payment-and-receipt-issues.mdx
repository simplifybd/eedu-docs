Questions about money deserve fast answers. Here's how to check any payment's status, what to do when a parent "paid but it's not showing", and how to verify a receipt.

---

## 1. Check a payment's status

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Finance → Payments`**.
2. Use the filters — **student, date, method, status** — to find the payment.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Completed / Paid** | Money confirmed; fees updated and receipt issued |
| **Pending** | Started but not yet confirmed by the gateway |
| **Failed / Cancelled** | Gateway rejected; nothing was recorded |
| **Partially Paid** | Some of the bill covered; balance still due |

### The student view (summary)

`Dashboard → Finance → Statements` shows each student's total due vs paid. A bill only turns **Paid** when a payment covers it fully.

---

## 2. "Parent paid online but my screen says Pending"

This is normal and resolves itself:

1. eEdu waits for the gateway to confirm, then marks **Completed** and creates the receipt automatically.
2. If the parent closed the browser mid-payment, click **Check / Verify Payment** next to the pending payment (or open the student's statement and press the same) — eEdu asks the gateway directly and settles it instantly.
3. If it still says Pending after that, the gateway itself is slow (some banks take minutes). Open the **payment history again in 5–10 minutes**.

> [!NOTE]
> A payment the gateway reports as **failed** is removed completely — it is never counted, so the student's balance just stays open.

> [!WARNING]
> Never mark a parent's payment "paid" manually while the gateway shows Pending — the same fee could then be collected twice when the gateway reply arrives. Wait for the official status.

## 3. A parent shows you a payment screenshot — how do I verify it?

Two trustworthy checks only:

### Via eEdu

1. Open `Dashboard → Finance → Payments` and search by the **payment reference / tracking number** from the screenshot.
2. Confirm it shows **Completed** and the correct **student + amount**.

### Via the receipt QR (works without login)

1. Scan the QR code on the printed/PDF receipt with any phone camera.
2. It opens the **public verification page** showing receipt number, amount, date, institute and student, with a **Verified** confirmation.
3. If the page says **"signature invalid — this receipt may be forged"**, the receipt was altered — issue a correct one and investigate.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** *Verification page showing a checkmark, receipt number and amount.*

> [!TIP]
> Accept a QR-scanned "Verified" result as proof — it's generated by the same server that holds your ledger. A screenshot of an app screen is not proof.

---

## 4. Receipt lost? Reprint it

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Finance → Receipts`**.
2. Search by **receipt number**, student or date.
3. Click **Print / Download** for a fresh PDF with the same valid QR.

Receipt numbers look like `RCT-20261006-1A2B3C4D` — stable and unique per collection.

---

## 5. Correcting mistakes (void & re-issue)

- **Wrong amount collected?** Void the payment (available on the payment row), then collect again. The fee balance returns to what it should be.
- **Receipt cancelled?** Voiding records the cancellation in the audit log — never just delete, so your totals stay explainable.
- **Partial payment then full?** Partially-paid bills simply stay open; the next payment finishes them to **Paid**.

---

## 6. Bank / wallet "money taken but no receipt yet"

1. Confirm in the gateway's own app that the charge actually went through.
2. If it did: use **Check / Verify Payment** — eEdu reconciles with the gateway and finishes the settlement.
3. Only if eEdu still can't match it after the gateway confirms: contact **eEdu support** with the payment date, amount and the gateway reference — they can reconcile it into your ledger.

> [!DANGER]
> Never pocket a charge and keep the fee "unpaid" just because the screen didn't refresh. Verify first — the money is in your merchant account; eEdu just needs a confirmation nudge.

## Quick reference

| I want to… | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| See today's collected money | `Finance → Payments` (filter by date) |
| Settle a stuck online payment | Click **Check / Verify Payment** |
| Prove a receipt is real | Scan its QR → public "Verified" page |
| Reprint a receipt | `Finance → Receipts` → search → Print |
| Fix a wrong collection | Void the payment, then re-collect |
| Check one student's balance | `Finance → Statements` |
