description: Step-by-step fixes for punches that never arrive, arrive late, or land on the wrong day or person.
---

"Arrived and punched, but attendance says absent" — here's the fastest path to a fix. Work through the sections in order.

---

## 1. Did the punch reach eEdu at all?

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Students → Attendance → Settings → Devices`**.
2. Open the device's **Punch Logs** and set the filter to today.

| You see | Meaning | Next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Punch listed | eEdu received it | go to section 2 |
| Punch listed but **Error** | Received but couldn't match a person | go to section 4 |
| Nothing listed | Punch never arrived | go to section 5 (device side) |

## 2. Punch arrived — why is the register still wrong?

### Check the date rules

- **The register only counts the class's day.** Open `Dashboard → Students → Attendance` and confirm you're viewing the right **classroom**, **shift** (Morning/Day/Evening) and **date**.
- **Punch before the day's start time** (e.g. 8:30) is morning time, not the previous day — unless the device clock was wrong earlier.
- **Today isn't final yet.** Present/Late decisions settle as the day goes; `ABSENT` for a past day is completed by the automatic absent-check (which never marks *today* prematurely).

### Check for "Late" status instead of "Present"

A punch after the class's **late cutoff** time marks the student **Late** (still attendance). View the column — the student may actually be there, just Late:

`Dashboard → Students → Attendance → Settings` shows your school's **start time / late cutoff / grace minutes**. For example: start 08:30, cutoff 09:00 (+ 5 grace) means 09:02 → **Late**, 09:06 → you can leave for the automatic absent-check to decide.

## 3. The day looks half-empty

If only some students appear:

1. Confirm those students are **enrolled in the class for the active academic year** (`Dashboard → Students → Enrollments`).
2. Confirm the device **target group** includes students (see the mapping guide) — a teacher-scoped device won't match student IDs.
3. In **period-wise** mode, only students with a **routine period** that day are expected to appear; someone with no period that weekday is correctly skipped.

---

## 4. Punches show "Error" (unmatched)

The user ID on the device doesn't match anyone in eEdu:

1. Open the error row — note the **device user ID**.
2. Compare with the student's **admission number** / teacher's **employee ID** (`Dashboard → Students` / `Dashboard → Teachers`).
3. Fix the match:
   - Add the missing student/teacher, **or**
   - Re-number the user ID on the device.
4. Re-punch (or re-send the buffered log). Once matched, the record processes normally and any earlier absence is corrected automatically.

Errors are harmless to ignore while fixing — they don't create fake absences.

---

## 5. Nothing arrived at all (device side)

Head to the device-focused guide — this covers push mode, server address, secret key, clock and network in depth:

→ **[Device Offline & Sync Troubleshooting](/docs/hardware-guide/offline-and-sync-troubleshooting)**

Two-minute version: check **push mode** is on, **server = `api.eedu.bd` / port 443**, **secret key** matches, **internet** works, **device clock = Asia/Dhaka**. While the network is down, the device **buffers** punches and sends them automatically on reconnect — so before doing anything, wait a few minutes after the network returns.

---

## 6. Backdated repairs

Need to fix a past day by hand?

1. Open that day's register in `Dashboard → Students → Attendance`.
2. Mark individuals **Present/Late** (capped at the allowed **back-date limit**, usually 7 days — for older days, contact your eEdu support contact).
3. A future date can't be marked — correct the date first.

> [!WARNING]
> Don't mark *today* as a repair for "the device was off this morning" — a correct punch later automatically upgrades the day. If the device is still down, a manual Present mark is the right tool.

---

## 7. Speeding up "sync" when staff are anxious

- **Punches process automatically within minutes** — a large buffer drains in bursts (about 1,000 records every cycle). If the buffer count keeps climbing for over an hour, contact support.
- **Guardian alerts** for absences are sent on WhatsApp → SMS → in-app, in that order. If a parent got nothing, check the school's **notification toggles** (`Attendance settings → Guardian SMS on`) before assuming the absent was wrong.

| Absent looks wrong | Wrong enrollment/academic year or unapproved leave |
