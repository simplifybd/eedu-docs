---
title: Connect Your School's Own Domain
description: Point your own domain at your eEdu school website by adding a CNAME and TXT records at your domain registrar.
---

Your school website already works at `yourschool.eedu.bd`. This guide puts it on **your own domain** — for example `www.dhakamodel.edu.bd` — in four steps. No static IPs, no servers to buy.

> [!NOTE]
> A custom domain is a paid (Pro) feature. If you don't see the **Domain** menu, contact your eEdu account manager to enable it.

## What you'll need

- Admin access to eEdu (owner account).
- The login to your **domain registrar** account — wherever you bought the domain (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Hostinger, a .bd registrar, etc.).
- The CNAME and TXT values that eEdu shows you (they're generated automatically).

---

## Step 1 — Add the domain in eEdu

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Settings → Website/Domain`**.
2. Click **Add Domain**.
3. Type your full domain (e.g. `www.dhakamodel.edu.bd` or `dhakamodel.edu.bd`) and save.

eEdu now prepares the domain and shows you a **DNS setup table** — three records with exact values. Keep this screen open or copy the values.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** *The DNS records table showing three rows: CNAME, TXT (verification), TXT (SSL).*

> [!TIP]
> Typing the domain with or without `www.` is fine — eEdu handles both and redirects the root to www if needed.

---

## Step 2 — Add the DNS records at your registrar

Log in to your domain registrar and open **DNS / Name Servers / Manage DNS**. Add the records exactly as eEdu lists them.

### The three records to add

| Type | Host (name) | Value (points to / text) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CNAME** | `www` | `app.eedu.bd` |
| **TXT** | `_cf-custom-hostname` | the long verification text eEdu shows you |
| **TXT** | `_acme-challenge` | the second text value eEdu shows you |

> **Important for root domains:** registrars don't allow a CNAME on the bare domain (`dhakamodel.edu.bd` without www). So also add a **redirect** from the root to `www.dhakamodel.edu.bd` (most registrars have a "URL redirect" or "forwarding" option), or an **ALIAS record** at the root pointing to `www.dhakamodel.edu.bd`.

| Setting | Your value |
| --- | --- |
| CNAME host | `www` |
| CNAME value | `app.eedu.bd` |
| TXT host | `_cf-custom-hostname` (for subdomain domains: `_cf-custom-hostname.yourname`) |
| TXT value | paste from the eEdu DNS table — copy exactly, no extra spaces |
| TTL | leave default (or 300s if you can set it) |

> **Screenshot placeholder:** *Example registrar screen with the three DNS rows filled in.*

> [!TIP]
> TXT records must be entered **exactly** — a missing character or trailing space means verification will wait for a fix.

---

## Step 3 — Wait for the records to copy

DNS changes spread across the internet. Wait **15 minutes to an hour** (usually just a few minutes) before verifying.

---

## Step 4 — Verify the domain in eEdu

1. Back in **`Dashboard → Settings → Website/Domain`**, click **Verify** (or **Re-check**).
2. Status changes show in the domain panel:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | eEdu is waiting — records not all visible yet; wait a little and Verify again |
| **Active / Verified** | Done! Your site is now reachable at your own domain |

eEdu **automatically checks every hour** even if you don't click Verify, and finishes the setup itself once it detects the records.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** *Domain status panel before (Pending) and after (Active/Verified).*

---

## Step 5 — Enjoy your branded site

Visit `https://www.yourdomain` (clear your browser cache if it shows an old page). You should see your school's website with the address bar showing your own domain and a padlock.

Your old `yourschool.eedu.bd` address keeps working too — both lead to the same site.

---

## Quick checklist

1. Added the domain in eEdu and copied the three values.
2. CNAME `www → app.eedu.bd` added at the registrar.
3. Both TXT records added **exactly** as shown.
4. Root domain redirect (`example.com → www.example.com`) added.
5. Clicked **Verify** (or waited up to an hour for auto-verification).

> [!NOTE]
> Don't delete the TXT records after approval — eEdu keeps the free SSL certificate renewed automatically as long as the verification records stay in place.
