Want to see eEdu before creating an account? Open the **[live demo](https://demo.eedu.bd)** — a sample school with students, staff, fees, exams and reports already filled in. No sign-up or installation needed.

## Pick a role

| Role | What you can explore |
| --- | --- |
| **Principal / Admin** | Students, staff, fees, exams, payroll and institute-wide reports |
| **Teacher** | Class routine, attendance, homework, marks entry and exam duties |
| **Parent** | Children's attendance, results, fees, homework and notices |
| **Student** | Routine, results, homework, library and fee status |

Use the **role buttons** in the toolbar to switch views at any time.

## Good to know

- Sensitive actions are **turned off** in the demo: online payments, SMS, email and account settings cannot be changed, so nothing is sent or charged.
- Sample data **resets automatically every 6 hours**. To restore it yourself, choose **Reset demo** in the toolbar.
- Everything you see belongs to the sample school, not to your institution.
