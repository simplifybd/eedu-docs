---
title: Connect Your Own Domain
description: Point your domain at eEdu with a CNAME and two TXT records — no static IP or server needed.
---

<Figure src="/images/docs/diagram-domain.svg" alt="Domain verification flow: add domain, add DNS records, eEdu verifies, SSL issued" caption="Four steps from your domain to a live, secure site." />

Your site already runs at `yourschool.eedu.bd`. This guide puts it on **your own domain**, e.g. `www.dhakamodel.edu.bd`.

> [!NOTE]
> Custom domains are a paid (Pro) feature. If you don't see the **Domain** menu, ask your eEdu account manager to enable it.

**You need:** an owner/admin login and access to your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Hostinger, a .bd registrar…).

---

## 1. Add the domain in eEdu

`Dashboard → Settings → Domain` → **Add Domain** → enter the full domain → Save.

eEdu shows a **DNS setup table**. Keep it open — you'll copy the values.

---

## 2. Add the DNS records at your registrar

Open your registrar's **DNS / Manage DNS** page and add exactly what eEdu shows:

| Type | Host | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CNAME** | `www` | `app.eedu.bd` |
| **TXT** | `_cf-custom-hostname` | the long value from eEdu |
| **TXT** | `_acme-challenge` | the second value from eEdu |

- For a **root domain** (no `www`), add a **redirect** to `www` (registrars don't allow CNAME at root).
- **TTL:** leave default, or 300s.
- Copy TXT values **exactly** — one extra space blocks verification.

---

## 3. Wait, then verify

1. Wait **15–60 minutes** for DNS to spread.
2. Back in **`Dashboard → Settings → Domain`**, click **Verify**.
3. Status becomes **Active / Verified**. eEdu also re-checks automatically every hour.

---

## 4. Open your new address

Visit `https://www.yourdomain` (hard-refresh if you see the old page). Your eEdu subdomain keeps working too.

> [!NOTE]
> Don't delete the TXT records after launch — they renew your free SSL certificate automatically.
