Every institute has a public site at `yourschool.eedu.bd`. You build it from **`Dashboard → Settings → Website`**.

---

## 1. Choose a template

1. Open **Templates** and pick a starting look: **Essential** (simple), **Luminary** (modern), or **Royal Academy** (formal).
2. Apply it, then set colours and logo under **Theme**.

---

## 2. Edit your pages

1. Open a page (Home, About, Courses, Faculty, Gallery, Contact, FAQ, Blog…) or add a new one.
2. Drag in **blocks** (headline, text, image, notice list, admission form) and edit inline.
3. **Preview** on desktop and mobile, then **Publish**.

> [!TIP]
> Build while unpublished, then flip the site live when it's ready.

---

## 3. Turn on live sections

These pull real data instead of static text:

| Section | Source |
| --- | --- |
| **Notice Board / Announcements** | content you publish |
| **Exam Results** | published results (searchable, captcha-protected) |
| **Class / Exam Routine** | published routines (PDF) |
| **Our Faculty** | teachers marked “Show on website” |
| **Gallery** | your media library |
| **Online Admission** | your admission form |

> [!NOTE]
> Only **published** content appears publicly — drafts stay private.

---

## 4. Online admission

Add the **Admission** section and design its fields. Applicants submit from your public site and get a receipt; you review and update their status in the dashboard.

---

## 5. Put it on your domain

When ready, connect a custom domain: **`Dashboard → Settings → Domain`** → follow [Connect Your Domain](/docs/website-and-domain/connect-your-domain).

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Changes not showing | Click **Publish**, then hard-refresh |
| A section is empty | It needs published data (notice, result, routine) |
| Admission form missing | Enable the Admission section (may need a plan) |
| Domain not working | See [SSL & verification](/docs/website-and-domain/ssl-and-verification) |
