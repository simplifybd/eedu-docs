title: Build Your School Website
description: Pick a template, edit pages with the built-in editor, publish notices and admissions, and launch on your own domain.
---

Every institute already has a public website at `yourschool.eedu.bd`. This guide shows how to build and publish it from the **Website** editor — no coding, no separate hosting.

---

## Part 1 — Open the website builder

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Settings → Website`**.
2. You land in the **page editor**. Across the top you can manage **Pages**, **Theme**, and **Templates**.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** *The Website editor with a live page preview and a sidebar of blocks/sections.*

---

## Part 2 — Choose a template

1. Open **Templates** and pick a starting design:

| Template | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Essential** | a clean, simple school site |
| **Luminary** | a modern, feature-rich site |
| **Royal Academy** | a formal, academic look |

2. Apply it, then customise colours and the logo under **Theme**.

---

## Part 3 — Edit your pages

1. Open a page (Home, About Us, Academic Programs, Faculty, Gallery, Contact, FAQ, Blog …) or **add** a new one.
2. Drag **blocks/sections** (headline, text, image, notice list, admission form, gallery) and edit their content inline.
3. Set each page's **title** and whether it appears in the site menu.
4. **Preview** to check on desktop and mobile, then **Publish**.

> [!TIP]
> You can build the site while it stays unpublished, then flip it live when it is ready.

---

## Part 4 — Turn on the dynamic sections

Some sections pull live data from your dashboard instead of static text:

| Section | Where the content comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Notice Board** | website notices you publish |
| **Announcements** | announcements you publish |
| **Exam Results** | published results (searchable by roll, captcha-protected) |
| **Class / Exam Routine** | published routines (downloadable PDF) |
| **Our Faculty** | teachers marked "Show on website" |
| **Gallery** | images from your media library |
| **Online Admission** | the admission form you design |

Enable the ones you want under the website settings/section toggles. Sections that belong to a paid plan may need that plan active.

> [!NOTE]
> Only content you explicitly **publish** appears on the public site — drafts stay private.

---

## Part 5 — Online admission form

1. Add the **Admission** section and design the fields you want to collect.
2. Applicants fill the form from your public site (protected by captcha) and receive a receipt.
3. Their applications appear in the dashboard under admissions, where you can review and update their status.

---

## Part 6 — Put it on your own domain

Once the site looks right, connect a custom domain so visitors see `www.yourschool.edu.bd` instead of the eEdu subdomain:

- **`Dashboard → Settings → Domain`** — claim your domain and follow the DNS steps.
- Full walkthrough: [Connect Your Domain (CNAME & TXT)](/docs/website-and-domain/connect-your-domain) and [Free SSL & Verification](/docs/website-and-domain/ssl-and-verification).

The eEdu subdomain keeps working alongside your custom domain.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Changes not showing | Click **Publish**; hard-refresh the browser to clear cache |
| A section stays empty | It needs published data (notice, result, routine…) |
| Admission form missing | Enable the Admission section; some sections require the relevant plan |
| Domain not resolving | See [SSL & verification](/docs/website-and-domain/ssl-and-verification) |
