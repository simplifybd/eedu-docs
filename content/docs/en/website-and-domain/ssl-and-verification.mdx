When you connect a custom domain, eEdu issues a **free SSL certificate** automatically. That gives visitors the padlock (`https://`) and keeps the connection secure.

---

## How it works

1. You add the **CNAME** and **TXT** records (see [Connect Your Domain](/docs/website-and-domain/connect-your-domain)).
2. eEdu verifies ownership and the two `_acme-challenge` / `_cf-custom-hostname` records.
3. A certificate is issued and **renews itself** as long as the TXT records stay in place.

---

## Check your setup

1. Open **`Dashboard → Settings → Domain`**.
2. Confirm the status is **Active / Verified**.
3. Open `https://www.yourdomain` in a browser:
   - **Padlock visible** → all good.
   - **“Not secure”** → certificate not issued yet; wait a few minutes and retry.

---

## Common problems

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Status stuck on **Pending** | Re-check the records are copied exactly; DNS may still be spreading |
| Site opens but no padlock | Wait for issuance, or re-run **Verify** |
| TXT values keep failing | Remove trailing spaces; ensure you used the correct Host |
| Certificate not renewing | You deleted the TXT records — restore them |
| Wrong site on the domain | Confirm the CNAME points to `app.eedu.bd` |
