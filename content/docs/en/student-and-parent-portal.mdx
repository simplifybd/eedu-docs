Alongside the school dashboard, eEdu gives every student and guardian their own login. This guide is written for **families** — share it with them so they know what they can do without calling the office.

---

## Logging in

1. Open your school's address (e.g. `yourschool.eedu.bd`) or the eEdu login page.
2. Sign in with the email/phone and password given by the school.
3. Students land on the **student dashboard**; guardians land on the **parent dashboard**.

> [!NOTE]
> Forgot the password? Use **Forgot password** on the login screen. The school office does not need to reset it manually.

---

## Student portal

A student sees everything for their own account:

| Menu | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | upcoming homework, notices and a quick summary |
| **Academics → Attendance** | their attendance record and calendar |
| **Academics → Timetable** | class routine and exam routine |
| **Academics → Homework** | assignments, attachments, due dates and submission |
| **Academics → Results** | marksheets and report cards |
| **Academics → Analytics** | performance/progress view |
| **Finance → Fees** | fee statement and **Pay Online** |
| **Finance → Payment History** | receipts for past payments |
| **Communication** | notices, announcements and notifications |
| **Communication → Leave Request** | apply for leave and track approval |
| **Account** | profile and health records |

## Parent portal

A guardian can follow **one or more children** from a single login.

1. Use the **child switcher** at the top to change which child you are viewing.
2. The menu mirrors the student portal, all scoped to the selected child:

| Menu | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | the selected child's overview |
| **Academics** | attendance, timetable, homework and results |
| **Finance** | fees (with **Pay Online**) and payment history |
| **Communication** | notices, announcements, notifications |
| **Communication → Leave Request** | apply for leave for the child |

## Paying fees online

If the school has enabled online payment:

1. Go to **Finance → Fees** and open the unpaid month.
2. Click **Pay Online** and choose a method (bKash / Nagad / card / bank — whatever the school offers).
3. Complete the payment on the gateway page.
4. The fee is marked **Paid** and a **QR receipt** is created automatically.

> [!TIP]
> If a payment is interrupted, don't pay twice — check **Payment History** after a few minutes. See [Payment & receipt issues](/docs/troubleshooting/payment-and-receipt-issues).

---

## Downloading report cards, ID cards & certificates

- **Report cards** download from **Academics → Results** for each published exam term.
- **ID cards** and other documents are available from the profile/account area where the school has enabled them.

---

## Leave requests

1. Open **Communication → Leave Request**.
2. Choose the dates and give a short reason, then submit.
3. The class teacher/office sees the request in the school dashboard and approves or rejects it.
4. The status updates in the same list.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Can't see a child | Ask the school to link the guardian to the student's record |
| Results or routine missing | They may not be published yet |
| "Pay Online" not shown | Online payment is not enabled by the school |
| Wrong name/class shown | Contact the school office to correct the student record |
