---
title: Homework & Assignments
description: Assign homework to a class or subject, attach files, track who submitted, and grade submissions — visible to students and parents.
---

Homework flows straight from the teacher to the student portal. When you post an assignment, it appears on the student's and parent's dashboard; when students submit, you see it here to review and grade.

---

## Part 1 — Create homework

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Students → Homework`**.
2. Click **Add Homework** and fill in:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | e.g. `Algebra exercise 4.2` |
| **Classroom** | the class that must do it |
| **Subject** | which subject it belongs to |
| **Description** | the instruction shown to students |
| **Due date** | shown to students and parents as the deadline |
| **Attachment** | optional worksheet, image or PDF |

3. Click **Save** (or **Publish**). It is instantly visible in the student and parent portals.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** *The Add Homework form with classroom, subject, due date and attachment fields.*

> [!TIP]
> Keep the title short and put details in the description. Parents see the title first in their feed.

---

## Part 2 — Track submissions

1. Open any homework to see the list of students.
2. Each row shows **Submitted** or **Pending** (with the submission time).
3. Click a student to view or download what they uploaded.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** *Homework detail list with submitted/pending badges and attachment links.*

---

## Part 3 — Review and grade

1. Open a student's submission.
2. Add a **mark** and optional **feedback/comment**.
3. Save. The grade appears in the student's homework view and can flow into their overall record.

---

## Part 4 — What students and parents see

In the **student portal**, under **Academics → Homework**, a student can:

- See every assignment with its description and due date.
- Open attachments.
- Upload their submission before the deadline.

In the **parent portal**, the same list is shown per child under **Academics → Homework**, so families can follow up at home.

See the [Student & Parent Portal Guide](/docs/student-and-parent-portal) for the full walkthrough you can share with families.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Homework not visible to students | Confirm it was **saved/published** and the classroom is the active one |
| Student can't upload | Check their portal access and whether the due date has passed |
| Wrong subject list | Add the subject to the classroom in **`Dashboard → Students → Subjects`** first |
