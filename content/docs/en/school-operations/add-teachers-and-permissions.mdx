title: Adding Teachers & Managing Permissions
description: Create teacher records, grant dashboard access, send invites, and control what each person can see and do.
---

This guide covers bringing your staff onto eEdu: adding their records, giving them a login, and deciding which parts of the dashboard each person may use.

---

## Part 1 — Add a teacher's record

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Teachers`**.
2. Click **Add Teacher** and fill in the form:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Full name** | as it should appear on registers and payslips |
| **Designation / Department** | e.g. Assistant Teacher · Science |
| **Phone / Email** | email is required if you plan to grant dashboard access |
| **Employee ID** | leave blank — eEdu generates it automatically |
| **Joining date** | first working day (used on the profile) |
| **Show on website** | tick to list this teacher on your public site's "Our Teachers" page |
| **Grant dashboard access** | tick to give them a login (next part) |

3. Click **Save**. The teacher now appears in the list with their **Employee ID** (e.g. `000017`) — this is also the number used by biometric devices (see the [mapping guide](/docs/hardware-guide/user-id-mapping)).

> **Screenshot placeholder:** *The Add Teacher form with the main fields filled in.*

> [!TIP]
> Adding all teachers once at the start of the year (even before they join) lets you set up salary, leave and device access in advance.

## Part 2 — Give a teacher a login (dashboard access)

There are two ways, and eEdu picks the right one automatically:

- **If they already have an account** (they used eEdu before) → access is granted instantly; the teacher sees your school workspace the next time they log in.
- **If they don't have an account** → eEdu emails them an **invitation link**. They click it, create their password, and are automatically placed in your school's workspace.

To grant access:

1. Open the teacher's profile (**`Dashboard → Teachers`** → teacher row).
2. Choose **Dashboard access / Invite**.
3. Pick a **role / permission level** (see Part 3) and save.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** *"Grant dashboard access" dialog with role dropdown and permission toggles.*

The teacher accepts the invite link (valid for a limited time). If the link expires, just send a new invite from the same screen — no reason needed.

---

## Part 3 — Control what each teacher can do (permissions)

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Account → Members`** (owner account).
2. You'll see every member and every pending invite.

There are two levels of access:

| Level | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Write** | Can view **and** change that area (e.g. mark attendance, edit fees) |
| **Read** | Can view only (e.g. see the register but not edit it) |
| **Off** | Hidden (the menu item simply doesn't appear for them) |

Apply permissions:

1. Add or edit a member.
2. Tick or untick each feature — **Attendance**, **Fees**, **Results**, **Students**, **Reports**, etc.
3. Optionally limit a teacher to **specific classrooms**.
4. Save.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** *The permission matrix screen with features grouped and each row showing Off / Read / Write toggle buttons.*

> [!TIP]
> Start new teachers on **Read**, then widen to **Write** after a week. Most teachers need only: Attendance, Results/Exams, Homework, and their own Leave.

**Owner vs. Member:** the person who registered the institute is its **Owner** — the only one who can manage team members, billing and publishing results. Everyone else is a **Member** with the permissions you granted. Owners can hand some of these duties to a trusted **Head Teacher** by giving that member appropriate permissions.

---

## Part 4 — The "My Space" view (what teachers see)

Once connected, each teacher logs in to their own **My Space** (shown as their personal section of the dashboard). Here they can, without an admin:

- View their **profile**, salary structure and leave balance.
- See their **attendance** (punch times, present/late counts) and a monthly calendar.
- Request **leave** and see its approval status.
- Download their **payslip** (when payroll is run).
- See their **timetable**, classes and student lists.

Members can **never** see another teacher's salary or record leave on someone else's behalf — those stay with the owner.

> [!NOTE]
> Changing a teacher's permissions applies immediately. If someone "just lost access", save their permission toggles again and ask them to refresh the page.

---

## Part 5 — Removing access

When a teacher leaves:

1. Open the teacher's profile.
2. Choose **Archive** (or **Remove access** to keep the record but drop the login).
3. The email invites are revoked automatically.

Archived teachers hide from the active list but their attendance & salary history stays in reports. (Archived records are permanently removed from the system after 30 days, so archive with that in mind.)

> [!TIP]
> Re-archiving an "ex" teacher only restores their HR record — dashboard access is **not** restored automatically. If you want them back online, re-grant access from their profile.
