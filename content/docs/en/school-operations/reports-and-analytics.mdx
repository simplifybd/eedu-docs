`Dashboard → Reports` answers three questions: **how much did we collect**, **who still owes us**, and **how is attendance running**.

---

## 1. The headline numbers

Across the top of the page:

| Metric                 | Meaning                                                    |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Total students**     | active students in the current view                        |
| **Total collection**   | money received, with the change vs the previous period     |
| **Total due**          | outstanding fee items and how many students they belong to |
| **Collection rate**    | collected ÷ total payable                                  |
| **Attendance average** | attendance over the selected date range                    |

Use the **date range** control to compare a month, a term or a year.

---

## 2. Financial tab

- **Monthly collection** chart for the year — spot the months that dip.
- **Fee Categories** chart — outstanding money by fee type, so you can chase transport or tuition separately.
- **Month-by-Month Breakdown** table with totals and percentage of the year.
- **Export CSV** for the spreadsheet your accountant wants.

---

## 3. Due list

The due table sits with the financial report and shows **unpaid / partially paid fees**, filtered by classroom and month.

This is the list to work from for fee reminders — each row is a real outstanding amount, and it has its own **Export CSV**.

---

## 4. Attendance tab

**Attendance Summary** for the selected period: **Average Rate**, **Active Students**, **Total Classrooms**, collection rate and student status distribution. Read it alongside the due list to find students whose fees _and_ attendance are both slipping.

---

## 5. Student analytics (student portal)

Students and parents get their own view at `Dashboard → Academics → Analytics`:

- KPIs: **Attendance**, **Best GPA**, **Due**, **Health records**
- **Attendance trend**, **Exam performance**, **Fee overview**, **Subject performance**

It is read-only and reflects published data only.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem                     | Fix                                                    |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| “No collection data”        | No payments recorded in that range                     |
| Numbers look low            | Check the date range — it defaults to a shorter period |
| Student can't see analytics | Results/attendance not published yet                   |
| Need it in Excel            | **Export CSV** on the Financial tab and the Due list   |
