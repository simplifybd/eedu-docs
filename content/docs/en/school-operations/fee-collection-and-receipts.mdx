This guide covers the full money path: setting up fee items, collecting at the counter, printing a QR receipt, and letting parents pay online.

---

## Part 1 — Set up the fee items

### Fee categories

eEdu provides 18 built-in fee heads to choose from:

| Head | Head | Head |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tuition Fee | Admission Fee | Exam Fee |
| Sport Fee | Transport Fee | Library Fee |
| Hostel Fee | Uniform Fee | Stationary Fee |
| Laboratory Fee | Development Fee | Annual Fee |
| Re-Admission Fee | IT Fee | Medical Fee |
| Excursion Fee | Late Fine | Custom Fee |

### Add a fee to a student

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Finance → Student Fees`** (or `Dashboard → Students → Fees`).
2. Click **Add Fee**, choose the student and category:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Category / Label** | e.g. Tuition Fee |
| **Amount** | the headline charge |
| **Discount** | fixed amount or percentage (0–100%) |
| **Fine** | optional extra, e.g. late fine |
| **Month / Year** | which billing period this covers |
| **Due date** | shown to the parent |

3. Save. The bill appears on the student's fee statement.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** *Add Fee form with Amount, Discount and Due date fields.*

### Recurring monthly fees (use a template instead)

For monthly bills (the common case):

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Finance → Fee Templates`** → **New Template**.
2. Name it (e.g. `Class 6 monthly`) and add items (Tuition ৳1500, Development ৳300, …).
3. Set **Generate on day** (e.g. `1`) and **Due after day** (e.g. `10`).
4. Choose which classroom it applies to, and save.

Every month on the trigger day, eEdu **automatically creates** that month's fee rows for each enrolled student. You still collect them in Part 2.

> [!TIP]
> Set the due offset to 10–15 so parents who pay on the 1st aren't immediately overdue.

## Part 2 — Collect fees (cash counter)

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Finance → Collect Payment`** (or **Statements**).
2. **Search the student** by name or admission number.
3. The unpaid fees show in a list — **tick the ones being paid**.
4. Enter the **amount received** and the **payment method** (Cash, Bank, Mobile Banking, Cheque, Card/Online, Wallet, or **Free** for no-charge bills).
5. Click **Pay**.

What happens automatically:

- A **receipt is created** and the fees turn **Paid** (or **Partially Paid** if the amount doesn't cover everything — the rest stays as a remaining balance).
- The **unchanged totals are locked** — two cashiers can't collect the same bill twice, even at the same moment.
- An **audit record** is saved showing exactly who collected from whom.

> [!WARNING]
> Each payment uses one method. If a parent pays half cash and half by wallet, collect it as two separate payments — this keeps the day's cash register totals clean.

**Overpayments:** the system refuses a payment larger than the remaining balance (`Payment amount exceeds total remaining due`). Collect the exact amount or less.

## Part 3 — Print the receipt (with QR code)

1. From the confirmation screen, click **Print / Download receipt**.
2. A PDF receipt opens with:
   - Institute name, logo and address.
   - **Receipt number** (e.g. `RCT-20261006-1A2B3C4D`), date, student and class.
   - Each fee line and the total paid.
   - A **QR code** in the corner.
3. Print it (the QR prints at any size) or send the PDF to the parent.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** *A sample receipt PDF with the QR code highlighted in the corner.*

**Already-paid lookups:** find any receipt later via **`Dashboard → Finance → Receipts`** — search by receipt number or student.

## Part 4 — Parents verify a receipt (QR)

Anyone (no login needed) can verify a printed receipt:

1. Scan the QR code with any phone camera.
2. It opens a **verification page** on the eEdu public site showing: receipt number, amount, date, institute and student.
3. If the receipt was altered or fake, the page shows **"signature invalid — this receipt may be forged"**.

> **Diagram placeholder:** *Printed receipt → phone scans QR → verification page shows "Verified" with checkmark.*

> [!TIP]
> Encourage the accounts office to verify large receipts by QR before accepting them elsewhere — it takes two seconds and stops photocopier fraud.

## Part 5 — Let parents pay online

1. First connect an online payment account: **`Dashboard → Finance → Payment Gateways`** → **Add** and choose bKash, Nagad, SSLCommerz, ShurjoPay, aamarPay, EPS or DGePay.
2. Enter the merchant credentials for that gateway and save.
3. Turn on online payment for the student/parent portal (`Settings → Finance`).

Parents now see a **"Pay Online"** button on their child's fee statement and can pay from home. When they do:

- Payment goes through **your institute's own merchant account** (money arrives in your bKash/bank, not eEdu).
- eEdu watches the gateway response, and once the gateway confirms success, marks the fee **Paid** and creates the same QR receipt.
- If the gateway says failed, nothing is recorded — no phantom half-paid bill.

> [!NOTE]
> The payment page is a redirect to the gateway (bKash/Nagad/etc.). If the parent's browser closes mid-payment, the pending payment check (see Troubleshooting) settles it automatically.

## Part 6 — Discounts & installment plans (handy for offices)

- **Waive part of a bill:** open the fee → set **Waived amount** → the bill becomes "Waived" if nothing remains. Great for scholarships and staff children.
- **Spread a big bill:** **`Dashboard → Finance → Installment plans`** → **New**. Choose the total, the number of installments (2–60) and a due day. eEdu splits it evenly into monthly fees — the last installment absorbs any rounding.

> [!TIP]
> Use a **Free (৳0)** payment for full scholarships: it creates a proper zero-receipt and audit trail instead of silently deleting the bill.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Student's bill not showing | Check month/year and confirm the fee is for the active classroom |
| Can't collect more than remaining | Collect the exact remaining amount, or waive/discount first |
| Online payment stuck "Pending" | eEdu settles it on return/verification; check the payment status (see [Payment status checks](/docs/troubleshooting/payment-and-receipt-issues)) |
| Receipt says "invalid" when scanned | Receipt was altered → issue a new receipt and void the old one |
| Template not generating | Confirm template is Active and the generation day passed |
