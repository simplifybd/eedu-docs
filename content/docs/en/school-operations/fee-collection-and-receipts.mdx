## 1. Set up the fee

**One-off:** `Dashboard → Students → Fees` → **Add Fee** → student, category, amount, discount, month/year, due date → Save.

**Monthly (recommended):** `Dashboard → Students → Fees → Templates` → **New Template** → add items (Tuition ৳1500, …), set **generate on day** and **due after day**, pick the classroom. eEdu then creates each month's bills automatically.

Built-in fee heads include Tuition, Admission, Exam, Transport, Hostel, Library, Development and more.

---

## 2. Collect at the counter

1. `Dashboard → Students → Payment History` → **Collect Payment** (or open the statement).
2. Search the student, **tick the bills being paid**.
3. Enter **amount received** and **method** (Cash, Bank, Mobile Banking, Cheque, Card/Online, Wallet, Free).
4. Click **Pay**.

The bill turns **Paid/Partial**, totals are locked against double collection, and an **audit record** is saved. Overpayments are refused — collect the exact remaining amount.

---

## 3. Print the QR receipt

From the confirmation, click **Print / Download receipt**. The PDF shows institute details, receipt number, each fee line, the total, and a **QR code**.

<Figure src="/images/docs/diagram-receipt.svg" alt="A receipt QR scanned by a phone to verify on eEdu" caption="Anyone can verify a receipt in two seconds — no login needed." />

---

## 4. Parents verify a receipt

Scan the QR with any phone camera → opens the eEdu verification page showing receipt number, amount, date, institute and student. A tampered or fake receipt shows **“signature invalid”**.

---

## 5. Let parents pay online

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Billing → Payment Gateway`**, add a provider (bKash / Nagad / SSLCommerz / DGePay …), and enter your merchant credentials.
2. Enable online payment for the student/parent portal.

Parents then see **Pay Online** on the fee statement. Money goes to **your own account**; eEdu marks the fee paid and creates the same QR receipt once the gateway confirms.

## Discounts & installments

- **Scholarship:** open the fee → set **Waived amount** (use a free ৳0 payment for a clean audit trail).
- **Spread a big bill:** `Dashboard → Students → Fees → Installment Plans` → split into 2–60 monthly installments.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Bill not showing | Check month/year and active classroom |
| “Exceeds remaining due” | Collect less, or waive/discount first |
| Online payment stuck | eEdu settles on return — see [payment issues](/docs/troubleshooting/payment-and-receipt-issues) |
| Receipt shows invalid | Issue a new receipt and void the old one |
