The exam workflow has a clear rhythm: **set up the exam → enter marks → lock & generate → publish**. Follow it in order and result day practically runs itself.

---

## Part 1 — Set up the exam term

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Students → Exams & Results`** (also called **Exam Terms**).
2. Click **Add Exam Term** and name it — e.g. `Half Yearly 2026`, `Final Exam`, `Test Exam`.
3. (Optional) add a description; save.

> [!NOTE]
> Terms are labels you control. Reuse the same names each year ("Final 2026", "Final 2027") so result trends stay comparable.

### Book the exam schedule (rooms & dates)

1. In **Exam Schedules**, click **Add Schedule**.
2. Pick the **subject paper**, **classroom**, **date**, **start/end time** and **room**.
3. Optionally assign an **invigilator** (a teacher from your staff list).
4. Save. The printable **Exam Routine** is available from the same screen.

---

## Part 2 — Prepare subject mark sheets

Before entering marks, make sure each subject has its **components** set (see the [Timetable guide, Step 2](/docs/school-operations/class-routine-and-timetable)):

- **CQ** (creative), **MCQ** (multiple choice), **Practical**, **Viva** — each with **Total** and **Pass** marks.
- A typical setup: CQ 50/pass 15 + MCQ 25/pass 8.

If a component isn't enabled, marks for it are rejected — so double-check before exams start.

---

## Part 3 — Enter marks

1. Open **`Dashboard → Students → Exams & Results → Marks`**.
2. Pick the **classroom**, **exam term** and **academic year**.
3. The class roster loads with every student and subject. For each student, fill the boxes **CQ / MCQ / Practical / Viva** (the ones that apply to that subject).
4. Each student card shows which components are still **missing** — keep going until none remain.
5. Click **Save** (or **Save All**). Grades are calculated automatically — you type numbers, never letters.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** *The marks entry sheet showing a student row with CQ/MCQ/Practical/Viva input boxes and a "missing components" badge.*

> [!TIP]
> Enter marks as **scores**, not grades. The system derives the grade (A+ … F) from the mark using the national scale — so your printed report card and the board's grading always agree.

### Editing after entry

- Before publishing, open the student and correct any box, then save.
- Handy filter: the class view shows who has **incomplete** components.

---

## Part 4 — Generate the exam documents

From the same **Exams & Results** area (per classroom + term):

### Tabulation Sheet
- Shows every student × every subject with marks, total, GPA and grade; failed rows are highlighted.
- Use it for the committee's review and your permanent record.

### Merit List
- Students ranked by **GPA → total marks → roll** (the same rule the board uses).
- Top 3 are highlighted (1st/2nd/3rd).

### Admit Cards
- Open **Admit Cards** and pick the classroom + term.
- Generate the PDF — each card shows student name, roll, class, the exam title, dates/room and the school header. Print in bulk and cut.

### Exam Routine & Report Cards
- **Routine**: the printable schedule from Part 1.
- **Report Card**: the bilingual (Bangla/English) card with the marks table, grades, GPA and PASSED/FAILED summary — ideal for the result day handout. An optional verification QR on the card lets parents confirm it online.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** *Thumbnails of the Tabulation Sheet, Merit List, Admit Card and Report Card PDFs.*

| Document | When you need it |
| --- | --- |
| Tabulation Sheet | committee review, records |
| Merit List | class rankings |
| Admit Card | before the exam starts |
| Report Card | result day handout |
| Exam Routine | students & parents |

## Part 5 — Publish results

Publishing makes results visible to students/parents (portal + app) and optional SMS/WhatsApp notice.

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Students → Exams & Results → Publish`**.
2. Choose the **classroom**, **term** and **academic year**.
3. Click **Publish**.

Publishing **locks** the session — once published, marks can't be edited (`"Cannot modify results of a published exam session"`). If you spot an error later:

1. **Unpublish** the session (back to draft).
2. Correct the marks.
3. **Republish**.

> [!WARNING]
> Publish only after the exam committee signs off. Republishing is possible but noisy — parents already saw the first version.

### Public result lookup (optional)

If enabled, parents can check results on your public website by **roll number** without a login. Everything shown is from the published session only.

## How grading works (quick reference)

| Marks % | Grade | Points (NCTB) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 80–100 | A+ | 5.00 |
| 70–79 | A | 4.00 |
| 60–69 | A- | 3.50 |
| 50–59 | B | 3.00 |
| 40–49 | C | 2.00 |
| 33–39 | D | 1.00 |
| below 33 | F | 0.00 |

**Important national rule — one fail is a fail:** if **any** subject is an `F`, the **overall GPA becomes 0.00** (report card shows FAILED). This matches board regulation and is applied automatically.

> University & polytechnic institutes use a **4.0 scale** automatically (A+ still needs 80+). No settings to change — it follows your institute type.

## Common questions

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Why is GPA 0.00 with one fail? | Board rule — one subject below 33 points fails the whole result |
| Can I change marks after publishing? | Unpublish → edit → republish |
| Can I generate admit cards before marks? | Yes — admit cards don't need marks |
| Do I type grades? | No — only marks; grades are automatic |
| Where do parents see results? | On their portal/app after publish |
