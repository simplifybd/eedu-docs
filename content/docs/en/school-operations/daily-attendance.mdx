---
title: Daily Attendance — Mark, Review & Alert
description: Take daily or period-wise attendance, review monthly calendars, export registers, and send absence alerts to guardians.
---

eEdu records attendance three ways — manually by the class teacher, automatically from biometric devices, or a mix of both. This guide covers the manual and review side; physical device setup lives in [Connecting Your Attendance Device](/docs/hardware-guide/device-cloud-config).

---

## Part 1 — Choose how attendance works

Set this up once before the term starts.

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Students → Attendance`**.
2. Open the **Settings** tab.
3. Decide the rules:

| Setting | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Marking mode** | One **daily** record per student, or **period-wise** (follows the class routine) |
| **Late cut-off** | The time after which a student is marked **Late** instead of **Present** |
| **Weekly holidays** | Days with no attendance (e.g. Friday) |
| **Shifts** | Morning / Day / Evening timings, if your school runs more than one |

4. If you use **shifts**, open **Attendance Shifts** and assign the correct shift to each classroom.

> [!TIP]
> Period-wise mode is best for high schools and colleges; primary schools usually need only one daily record.

---

## Part 2 — Mark attendance by hand

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Students → Attendance`** → **Mark Attendance** tab.
2. Pick the **classroom**, the **date**, and (for period-wise) the **period/shift**.
3. The class list appears. Mark each student:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Present** | On time |
| **Late** | Arrived after the late cut-off |
| **Absent** | Did not attend |
| **Leave** | Approved absence (see the leave guide) |

4. Use **Mark all present**, then change only the exceptions — it is the fastest way to take a register.
5. Add an optional note (e.g. `medical`) and click **Save**.

## Part 3 — Device punches and manual marks together

If biometric/RFID devices are installed, punches arrive automatically and the register is filled for you:

- A punch before the late cut-off → **Present**.
- A punch after the cut-off → **Late**.
- No punch → stays **Absent** for you to confirm.

You only need to open this screen to **correct exceptions** (forgot to punch, device offline, approved leave). Manual marks are never blocked by a late-arriving punch — the latest decision wins when you save.

> [!NOTE]
> A wrong device clock is the most common cause of odd Present/Late results. Confirm the device shows **Asia/Dhaka** with NTP on — see [Setting the device clock](/docs/hardware-guide/device-cloud-config#step-4--set-the-device-clock-very-important).

## Part 4 — Review, calendars and exports

From the same **Attendance** screen you can:

- See a **monthly calendar** per student (green/red day view).
- See a **class summary** (who is chronically absent).
- Run a **low-attendance report** for a date range.
- **Export** the register to CSV or PDF for filing or board submission.

## Part 5 — Absence alerts to guardians

When a student is absent, eEdu can tell the family automatically.

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Students → Absence Alerts`**.
2. Turn alerts on and choose the **channel** (SMS / WhatsApp / in-app notification).
3. Set how many days of absence trigger an alert, and the message text.
4. Send a one-off alert for a specific student from the same screen, or let it run on schedule.

> [!WARNING]
> Alerts are sent from your institute's own SMS/WhatsApp credit. Confirm your sender ID and balance before switching them on (see [Communication guide](/docs/school-operations/communication-and-notices)).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Can't save a register | The date may be a weekly holiday, or the teacher lacks **Write** permission for Attendance |
| Everyone shows Late | Late cut-off is too early, or the device clock is wrong |
| Device punches missing | Device offline or time wrong — see [Offline & sync troubleshooting](/docs/hardware-guide/offline-and-sync-troubleshooting) |
| Wrong class list | Student is not enrolled in that classroom for the active academic year |
