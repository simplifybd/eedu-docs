---
title: Notices, SMS & WhatsApp Messaging
description: Broadcast announcements to classes or the whole school, send SMS and WhatsApp messages, and use the in-app chat.
---

eEdu gives you four channels to reach people: **notices/announcements** inside the dashboard, **SMS**, **WhatsApp**, and **in-app messages**. Use in-app notices for logged-in users and SMS/WhatsApp to reach guardians who may not open the app.

---

## Part 1 — Send a notice or announcement

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Communication → Notifications`**.
2. Click **New Announcement** and fill in:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | e.g. `Annual sports day` |
| **Message** | the body text |
| **Audience** | the whole institute, or specific **classrooms** |
| **Channel** | in-app only, or also SMS / WhatsApp |
| **Schedule** | send now or pick a date/time |

3. Click **Send**. It appears under **Notices/Announcements** in the student and parent portals, and as an in-app **Notification**.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** *New Announcement form with audience selector and channel checkboxes.*

> [!TIP]
> Set the audience to a **classroom** for class-specific news; use whole-institute notices for holidays, exams and events.

---

## Part 2 — SMS

SMS works on prepaid credits and a registered **Sender ID**.

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Communication → SMS`**.
2. **Buy / recharge credits** and register your **Sender ID** (the name the receiver sees). New sender IDs are approved by eEdu.
3. Compose a message, choose recipients (class or all), and send. You can also send to a single number.
4. Track **delivery status**, orders and message logs from the same screen.

> [!WARNING]
> Keep an eye on the credit balance — attendance and absence alerts also draw from it. Recharge before exam or result season.

---

## Part 3 — WhatsApp

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Communication → WhatsApp`**.
2. **Connect** your school's WhatsApp account by scanning the QR code shown on screen.
3. Set up the tools you need:
   - **Auto-reply** for common questions.
   - **Message templates** for fees, results and notices.
   - **Contacts/groups** for bulk sends.
   - **Delivery reports** to see who received a message.
4. Send a notice over WhatsApp from the Announcement form (Part 1) or in bulk from here.

> [!NOTE]
> WhatsApp runs from your institute's own number. Keep the phone connected and the session alive; a long disconnection requires re-scanning the QR.

---

## Part 4 — In-app messages

The **Messages** screen (`Dashboard → Communication → Messages`) is a real-time chat with staff and portal users for quick back-and-forth that doesn't need a formal notice.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Sender ID not working | Approval may be pending — check the SMS screen status |
| SMS not delivered | Insufficient credits or the number is invalid |
| WhatsApp disconnected | Re-scan the QR code from the WhatsApp screen |
| Guardian did not get a notice | They may only receive SMS/WhatsApp if those channels were ticked |
