Use in-app notices for logged-in users, and SMS/WhatsApp to reach guardians who may not open the app.

---

## 1. Send a notice

`Dashboard → Communication → Notifications` → **New Announcement**:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Title / Message | your text |
| Audience | whole institute or a **classroom** |
| Channel | in-app, SMS, WhatsApp |
| Schedule | now or later |

It appears under **Notices/Announcements** in the student and parent portals.

---

## 2. SMS

`Dashboard → Communication → SMS`:

1. Buy/recharge **credits** and register a **Sender ID** (approved by eEdu).
2. Compose a message, choose recipients (class or all), send.
3. Track **delivery status**, orders and logs.

> [!WARNING]
> Attendance and absence alerts also draw from the same credit — recharge before exam season.

---

## 3. WhatsApp

`Dashboard → Communication → WhatsApp`:

1. **Connect** your school's WhatsApp by scanning the QR.
2. Set up **auto-reply**, **templates**, **contacts/groups** and **delivery reports**.
3. Send notices from the Announcement form or in bulk here.

> [!NOTE]
> WhatsApp runs from your own number. Keep the phone connected; a long disconnect needs a re-scan.

---

## 4. In-app chat

`Dashboard → Communication → Messages` is real-time chat with staff and portal users.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Sender ID not working | Approval may be pending |
| SMS not delivered | Low credit or invalid number |
| WhatsApp disconnected | Re-scan the QR |
| Guardian didn't get it | Tick the SMS/WhatsApp channel when sending |
