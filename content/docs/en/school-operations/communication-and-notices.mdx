Use in-app notices for logged-in users, and SMS to reach guardians who may not open the app.

---

## 1. Send a notice

`Dashboard → Website → Announcements` → **Add Announcement** (create notices under `Website → Notices`, or use **Notify classroom** on a timetable entry):

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Title / Message | your text |
| Audience | whole institute or a **classroom** |
| Channel | in-app, SMS |
| Schedule | now or later |

It appears under **Notices / Announcements** in the student and parent portals. `Dashboard → Communication → Notifications` is only the inbox for reading and clearing them.

---

## 2. SMS

`Dashboard → Communication → SMS`:

1. Buy/recharge **credits** and register a **Sender ID** (approved by eEdu).
2. Compose a message, choose recipients (class or all), send.
3. Track **delivery status**, orders and logs.

> [!WARNING]
> Attendance and absence alerts also draw from the same credit — recharge before exam season.

---

## 3. In-app chat

`Dashboard → Communication → Messages` is real-time chat with staff and portal users.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Sender ID not working | Approval may be pending |
| SMS not delivered | Low credit or invalid number |
| Guardian didn't get it | Tick the SMS channel when sending |
