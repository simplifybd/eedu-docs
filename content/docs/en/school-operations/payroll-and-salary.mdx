---
title: Staff Payroll, Salary & Loans
description: Set salary structures, run monthly payroll, pay staff, generate payslips, and manage advances and loans.
---

Payroll takes each teacher's **salary structure**, adds their attendance and deductions, and produces a payslip you can pay and print — all from the **Teachers → Salary** area.

---

## Part 1 — Set up salary structures

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Teachers`** → open a teacher → **Salary** tab (or **`Dashboard → Teachers → Salary`**).
2. Enter the **salary structure**:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Basic / Gross** | the headline monthly salary |
| **Allowances** | house rent, transport, etc. |
| **Deductions** | provident fund, tax, etc. |
| **Pay mode** | how this person is normally paid |

3. Save. Repeat for each staff member, or bulk-apply a structure by designation.

> [!TIP]
> Set the structure once, then only the month's variables (unpaid leave, overtime, bonus, loan installment) change at run time.

---

## Part 2 — Run the monthly payroll

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Teachers → Salary`**.
2. Choose the **month** and review the generated list.
3. eEdu pulls each person's **attendance** (days present, unpaid leave/overtime from the device or manual register) and applies it to the salary.
4. Adjust any individual line if needed (bonus, fine, advance recovery).
5. Click **Pay** (single or **Bulk Pay**) to mark the month as paid.

> **Screenshot placeholder:** *Monthly payroll list with net payable per teacher and a Bulk Pay button.*

> [!WARNING]
> Payroll is a money action. Double-check the month and any manual adjustments before **Bulk Pay** — corrections after payment are recorded as adjustments, not silent edits.

---

## Part 3 — Payslips & payment history

- Each teacher can download their own **payslip** from **My Space → My Salary**.
- As an admin, use **`Dashboard → Teachers → Salary → Payment History`** to look up any past payment and re-print its payslip.
- Payslips show earnings, deductions, attendance-derived figures and the net amount.

---

## Part 4 — Loans & advances

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Teachers → Salary → Loan Management`**.
2. Add a **loan/advance** for a staff member: amount, number of installments, and start month.
3. eEdu automatically deducts the installment from each month's payroll until it is repaid.
4. Staff can track the balance and repayment from **My Space → My Loans**.

---

## Part 5 — Leave quota

Before running payroll, make sure leave is configured so unpaid leave is calculated correctly.

1. Go to **`Dashboard → Teachers → Leave Quota`**.
2. Set each staff member's yearly **entitlement** per leave type.
3. Approved leave requests draw from this balance; anything beyond the paid quota is treated as unpaid.

See the staff leave workflow in the [Teachers & Permissions guide](/docs/school-operations/add-teachers-and-permissions).

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Salary wrong for one teacher | Check unpaid leave and any manual deduction line |
| Payslip not visible to staff | The month isn't marked paid yet, or they have no **My Space** access |
| Loan not deducting | Confirm the loan is active and the start month has arrived |
| Attendance not reflected | Device/manual attendance for the month is incomplete |
