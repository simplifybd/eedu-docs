Everything about money your institute pays eEdu lives under **`Dashboard → Billing`**: Wallet, Transactions, Invoices, My plan, Referrals and Payment Gateway.

> [!NOTE]
> **Wallet, Transactions and My plan are owner-only.** Regular staff members will not see them — ask the workspace owner if a change is needed.

---

## 1. See what your plan includes

`Dashboard → Billing → My plan` lists the **Free** and paid tiers with what each one unlocks. The sidebar footer shows a live **usage meter** (students and classrooms used vs. your limit) and a **Renew** button.

Three features are paid-tier only: **SMS credits**, **WhatsApp** and a **custom domain**. Everything else — students, attendance, exams, fees, timetable, library, reports — works on Free.

---

## 2. Upgrade or renew

1. `Dashboard → Billing → My plan` → choose the plan.
2. Have a discount? Click **Have a coupon code?**, type the code, **Apply** — the discount appears next to it. **View available offers** lists the live promotions.
3. Pay with **Pay Online** (bKash, Nagad, card…) or **Wallet Balance**.
4. Confirm — access unlocks immediately.

Members who are not the owner see **Contact your workspace admin to upgrade the plan** instead of the payment options.

> [!TIP]
> The sidebar **Renew** button is the fastest path when a plan has simply expired.

---

## 3. Top up the wallet

`Dashboard → Billing → Wallet` → **Top up**.

- Amounts between **৳500 and ৳50,000** per top-up.
- Balance pays for plan renewals and other eEdu charges without re-entering card details.
- Every top-up appears in **Transactions** with its source.

---

## 4. Invoices & transactions

| Page             | Shows                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Invoices**     | Invoice number, type (Subscription, Wallet Top-up, Salary, Refund), amount, status (Issued / Paid / Refunded / Void) and a **Download** PDF per row |
| **Transactions** | Every movement of money with status, source and type filters                                                                                        |

Use an invoice PDF as your accounting record; use Transactions to reconcile what actually cleared.

---

## 5. Referrals

`Dashboard → Billing → Referrals` holds your referral code and the rewards earned when another institute signs up with it. Share the code — credits land in your wallet when they qualify.

---

## 6. Payment gateway (parent online fees)

Setup for parents paying fees online lives in `Dashboard → Billing → Payment Gateway`: connect a provider (bKash, Nagad, SSLCommerz, ShurjoPay, ePS, aamarPay, DGePay) and enter your own merchant credentials.

Full steps are in [Student Fees & QR Receipts §5](/en/docs/school-operations/fee-collection-and-receipts).

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem                                | Fix                                                          |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Section says “unavailable”             | Your plan does not include it — open **My plan** and upgrade |
| “Access paused” banner                 | The workspace is past due — **Renew**                        |
| Staff member can't open Wallet/My plan | Owner-only — ask the owner                                   |
| Coupon won't apply                     | Expired, wrong plan, or already used                         |
| Gateway won't save                     | Check Client ID / Secret / API key with your provider        |
