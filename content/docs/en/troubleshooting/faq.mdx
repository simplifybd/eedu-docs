## Accounts & access

**Can one person work in more than one school on the same login?**
Yes. Use the **workspace switcher** (top-left of the dashboard) to move between schools you belong to. Permissions are separate per school.

**A teacher can't see a menu / "access denied".**
Their permission was set to **Read** or **Off** for that area. Ask the **owner** to open `Dashboard → Account → Members` and widen the feature. Members also can't approve leave or administer salary — that's owner-only by design.

**Invitation email expires / teacher didn't accept.**
Send a fresh invite from `Dashboard → Teachers → <teacher> → Dashboard access`. The new link works immediately.

**Who is the "Owner"?**
The person who registered the institute. Only the owner/billing access can manage team members, subscription and publishing. See [Teachers & Permissions](/docs/school-operations/add-teachers-and-permissions).

## Attendance & devices

**Device pushed punches but nothing shows.**
Check the device's **Punch Logs** — a **Processed** row should appear within minutes. If not, work through [Logs won't sync](/docs/troubleshooting/attendance-sync-issues). Devices buffer offline, so a reconnect usually catches everything up.

**Punches arrive with "Error".**
The user ID on the device isn't matched to a student/teacher. Fix the **user ID mapping** — see [Mapping user IDs](/docs/hardware-guide/user-id-mapping).

**Device clock is wrong / times land on the wrong day.**
Set the device to **Asia/Dhaka** and turn on NTP time sync. Then re-test. See [Device & Biometric Setup](/docs/hardware-guide/device-cloud-config).

**Can my school use a device bought for another school?**
Technically yes — register it as a new device and map fresh user IDs. Remember the serial belongs to one school; if it shows "already registered", contact support.

**Automatic absences look wrong for one student.**
First check: is the student actually **enrolled** for this academic year, does their **class** match the device, and is their **approved leave** recorded? A student with approved leave shows as **On Leave**, not absent.

## Fees & payments

**How do parents pay online?**
Connect a gateway first (`Dashboard → Finance → Payment Gateways`), then turn on online payment in settings. Parents pay through **your** bKash/Nagad/SSLCommerz merchant account. See [Fee Collection & QR Receipts](/docs/school-operations/fee-collection-and-receipts).

**Parent paid online but it's still Pending.**
Use **Check / Verify Payment** — eEdu asks the gateway and settles it. See [Payment status checks](/docs/troubleshooting/payment-and-receipt-issues).

**How do I verify a printed receipt is genuine?**
Scan its **QR code** — it opens a public verification page. An altered receipt shows "may be forged". See [Receipt verification](/docs/troubleshooting/payment-and-receipt-issues).

**Do fees generate automatically every month?**
If you use a **fee template** — yes, on the trigger day each month. See the template section of [Fee Collection](/docs/school-operations/fee-collection-and-receipts).

**Can part of a bill be waived?**
Yes — set a **waived amount** or discount on the fee; the bill becomes "Waived" if nothing remains. Big bills can also be split into monthly **installments** (2–60).

## Exams & results

**GPA shows 0.00 even though the student passed most subjects.**
One subject below the pass mark (F) **fails the whole result** — this matches Board rule. Check the failed subject in the tabulation sheet. See [Exams, Marks & Results](/docs/school-operations/exams-marks-and-results).

**Can I change a mark after results are published?**
Yes — **unpublish** the session, correct the mark, **republish**. Until you unpublish, edits are blocked on purpose.

**Where do admit cards come from?**
`Dashboard → Students → Exams & Results → Admit Cards`, pick the class + term, and download the PDFs in bulk. Admit cards don't need marks entered.

## Website & domain

**My domain shows "Pending" for a long time.**
Usually a missing or mistyped TXT record at your registrar. Re-open the DNS table, copy the values **exactly**, then click **Verify** in `Dashboard → Settings → Website/Domain`. See [Connect Your Domain](/docs/website-and-domain/connect-your-domain).

**Do I have to renew the SSL certificate myself?**
No — it's free and renews automatically. Just keep the two TXT records in place. See [Free SSL & Verification](/docs/website-and-domain/ssl-and-verification).

**Can I have both my own domain and the free one?**
Yes — `yourschool.eedu.bd` and `www.yourdomain` both serve the same website.

**How do parents check results on the website?**
If you publish results and enable the public results section, parents enter the **roll number** on the site's Results page — no login required.

## Security & privacy

**What happens to fingerprint data?**
Biometric scans stay **only on the device**. eEdu stores numbers (admission/employee ID) and never receives a fingerprint or face image. See [Device setup](/docs/hardware-guide/device-cloud-config).

**Is the Device Secret Key transferable?**
No. Treat it like a password. If you think it leaked, **rotate the key** in the device list — the old one stops working immediately.

**I forgot my password.**
Use **Forgot password** on the login screen (email or phone OTP). Cooldowns apply to too many OTP requests — wait a minute and retry.
