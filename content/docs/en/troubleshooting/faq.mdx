## Accounts & access

**One login for several schools?** Yes — use the **workspace switcher** in the dashboard. Permissions are separate per school.

**A teacher can't see a menu?** Their permission is **Read/Off**. The **owner** widens it in `Dashboard → Account → Members`. Only owners approve leave and run salary. See [Teachers & Permissions](/docs/school-operations/add-teachers-and-permissions).

**Invitation expired?** Open the teacher → **Dashboard access** → send a new invite.

**Who is the owner?** Whoever registered the institute — the only one who manages team, subscription and publishing.

## Attendance & devices

**Punches arrive but nothing shows?** Check the device's **Punch Logs** — a **Processed** row should appear within minutes, else see [Logs won't sync](/docs/troubleshooting/attendance-sync-issues).

**Punches show "Error"?** The user ID isn't matched — fix [mapping](/docs/hardware-guide/user-id-mapping).

**Wrong day/time?** Set the device to **Asia/Dhaka** + NTP — see [Device setup](/docs/hardware-guide/device-cloud-config).

**Absence looks wrong?** Check the student is enrolled, the class matches the device, and any **approved leave** is recorded (leave shows **On Leave**, not absent).

## Fees & payments

**How do parents pay online?** Connect a gateway first, then enable online payment — money goes to **your own** bKash/Nagad account. See [Fee Collection](/docs/school-operations/fee-collection-and-receipts).

**Payment stuck on Pending?** Click **Check / Verify Payment** — see [payment issues](/docs/troubleshooting/payment-and-receipt-issues).

**How do I verify a receipt?** Scan its QR — the public page shows **Verified**; an altered receipt says "may be forged".

**Do fees generate each month?** Yes, if you use a **fee template**.

**Can part of a bill be waived?** Yes — set a **waived amount**/discount; big bills can be split into **installments** (2–60).

## Exams & results

**GPA 0.00 despite passing most subjects?** One failed subject (F) fails the whole result (Board rule) — check the tabulation sheet. See [Exams & Results](/docs/school-operations/exams-marks-and-results).

**Change a mark after publishing?** Yes — **unpublish** → correct → **republish**.

**Where are admit cards?** `Exam Suite → Admit Cards` → pick class + term → bulk PDF.

## Website & domain

**Domain stuck on Pending?** Usually a missing/mistyped TXT record — copy exactly, then **Verify**. See [Connect Your Domain](/docs/website-and-domain/connect-your-domain).

**Renew SSL myself?** No — it's free and automatic; keep the TXT records. See [SSL & Verification](/docs/website-and-domain/ssl-and-verification).

**Own domain and free subdomain together?** Yes, both serve the same site.

## Security

**Where does fingerprint data go?** Scans stay **only on the device**; eEdu stores numbers (admission/employee ID), never a fingerprint or face image.

**Can I share the Device Secret Key?** No — treat it like a password. If unsure, **rotate** it.

**Forgot my password?** Use **Forgot password** on the login screen (email/phone OTP).
