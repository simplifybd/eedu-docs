## 1. Check a payment's status

`Dashboard → Students → Payment History` (or **Finance → Payments**) → filter by student, date, method, status.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Completed / Paid** | Money confirmed; fees updated, receipt issued |
| **Pending** | Started, gateway hasn't confirmed yet |
| **Failed / Cancelled** | Rejected — nothing recorded |
| **Partially Paid** | Some covered; balance still due |

---

## 2. "Parent paid but it shows Pending"

Usually normal and self-resolving:

1. eEdu marks it **Completed** once the gateway confirms.
2. If the browser closed mid-payment, click **Check / Verify Payment** beside the pending row — eEdu asks the gateway and settles it.
3. Still pending after a few minutes? Some banks are slow — retry in 5–10 minutes.

> [!WARNING]
> Never manually mark a payment "paid" while it shows **Pending** — when the gateway reply arrives you could collect the fee twice.

---

## 3. Verify a payment or receipt

**By reference:** search **Payment History** for the reference/tracking number from the parent's screenshot; confirm **Completed**, correct student and amount.

**By QR (no login needed):** scan the receipt QR with a phone camera → the public page shows receipt number, amount, date, institute and student, with **Verified**. An altered receipt shows **“signature invalid — this receipt may be forged”**.

> [!TIP]
> Accept a QR-scanned **Verified** result as proof — it comes from the same server as your ledger. An app screenshot is not proof.

## 4. Reprint a receipt

`Dashboard → Students → Payment History` (or **Receipts**) → search by receipt number, student or date → **Print / Download**. Numbers look like `RCT-20261006-1A2B3C4D`.

---

## 5. Fix mistakes

- **Wrong amount:** **Void** the payment, then collect again. Balances return to normal.
- **Voiding** is recorded in the audit log — never delete, so totals stay explainable.
- **Partial then full:** partially-paid bills stay open until the next payment finishes them.

## Quick reference

| I want to… | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| See today's collection | Payment History, filter by date |
| Settle a stuck payment | **Check / Verify Payment** |
| Prove a receipt is real | Scan its QR → public **Verified** page |
| Reprint a receipt | Search → Print |
| Fix a wrong collection | Void, then re-collect |
